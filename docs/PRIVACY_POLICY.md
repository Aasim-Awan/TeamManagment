# TeamFund Manager Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Publisher: S&Ainnovation

Privacy and support contact: sainnovation14@gmail.com

## Information used by the app

TeamFund Manager stores information you enter: team names, player names and optional phone numbers, match details, expenses, contributions, expense splits and settlements. These records are used to provide team administration, fixture scheduling and financial bookkeeping on your device.

## Local storage and security

The app works locally without an online account or team synchronization service. S&Ainnovation does not operate a server that receives your team records from this app. Records are stored in the app's local Android storage. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock; someone with access to your unlocked device may be able to view the records.

## Sharing and exports

You may choose to export financial reports as CSV or share match details through Android's share sheet. Selected receiving apps handle these copies under their own privacy policies. CSV reports may include player names and financial information. Choose recipients carefully. CSV exports do not preserve all team records and are not complete backups.

## Android backups

Android may back up or restore app data according to your device and account settings. Backup processing and retention are controlled by Android and your backup provider. Manage those settings separately on your device.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them or use Settings → Reset All Data. Deactivating a player preserves their existing financial history. Reset All Data removes the app's local teams, players, matches and financial records. It does not remove previously exported files, copies shared with others or device backups; delete or manage those copies through the relevant apps and backup settings. Uninstalling the app generally removes local app storage, but backup restoration may bring back backed-up records.

## Permissions and external services

File import uses Android's system file picker. Sharing uses Android's share sheet. Notification permission, if granted, allows local notifications; scheduled reminders are currently unavailable. Opening a policy or support link uses your chosen browser or email app. Those services may process connection information or messages under their own policies. The app does not access your address book to populate the roster.

## Payments, advertising and tracking

TeamFund records payment information for bookkeeping; it does not move money, process payments or request bank-account or payment-card credentials. This version has no advertising or analytics SDK enabled and does not send team records to an advertising or analytics service.

## Children and team records

Team managers are responsible for obtaining permission to enter another person's information, including parent or guardian permission when required. Enter only information needed for managing the team. The app does not offer online child accounts or public player profiles.

## Support enquiries

If you contact sainnovation14@gmail.com, S&Ainnovation receives the information you include in your message and uses it to answer the enquiry. Avoid sending player records or financial details unless needed. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the enquiry and meet applicable obligations; contact the same address to request deletion.

## Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new effective date. Review it when installing updates.

## Contact
