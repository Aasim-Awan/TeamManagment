## Full description

Spend less time on team administration. TeamFund Manager brings your player roster, fixtures and shared costs together in one clear view.

YOUR TEAM AT A GLANCE
See the available team fund, money received, this month's spending and pending dues. Switch between your teams without mixing their records.

PLAYERS AND BALANCES
Maintain a player roster with roles and optional phone numbers. Search by name or role, review player balances and preserve financial history when a player becomes inactive.

MATCHES AND RESULTS
Schedule fixtures with dates, start times and venues. Record results and scores, link expenses to matches and share fixture details through your preferred messaging app.

SHARED EXPENSES
Record venue, equipment, travel and other team costs. Choose the payer, split costs equally or enter custom shares, and add a missing player while entering an expense.

ALL EXPENSES IN ONE TIMELINE
Every expense, contribution, settlement and charge, grouped by month and day. Filter by type, month or player, and see each player's share, what they paid and what is still unpaid.

CONTRIBUTIONS AND SETTLEMENTS
Record team contributions and settlements, including partial payments. Set monthly dues, charge match fees to the playing XI and record fines. Review unpaid shares and settlement history.

REPORTS YOU CAN USE
Review financial summaries and category totals. Export CSV financial reports for your own records or share them with the people you choose. Import TeamFund CSV files; rows already recorded are skipped. CSV reports are not complete team backups.

LOCAL AND PRACTICAL
Your team records are stored on your Android device. No online account is required. Choose light, dark or system appearance, your team's currency and seasons.

TeamFund Manager is a team administration and bookkeeping tool. It does not transfer money, process payments, provide bank accounts or offer online team synchronization. This version does not send reminders or notifications. Android backup behavior depends on your device and account settings.
