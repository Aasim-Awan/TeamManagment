## Owner signing — required before upload

You chose to create your own upload keystore. No key or password is generated by this task. Keep the keystore and a secure backup outside the repository; `.jks`, `.keystore` and `keystore.properties` are ignored.

Set `KEYSTORE_PATH`, `STORE_PASSWORD` and `KEY_PASSWORD` locally in your release build environment. The default alias is `upload`; set `KEY_ALIAS` if yours differs. Do not paste passwords into chat or commit them.

Run `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:lintDebug :app:lintRelease :app:verifyPlaySigning :app:bundleRelease`.

The output is `app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`. Without all three signing environment variables, the release bundle is deliberately **unsigned and cannot be uploaded to Play**. An unsigned review bundle is not an upload-ready signed artifact.

Validate the signed bundle with JDK `jarsigner -verify -verbose -certs app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`. Confirm its signer and package/version before sending it to Play. Enroll in Play App Signing using your own account.

## Play Console owner steps

1. Create the app in your verified developer account and confirm app name, language, category, free/paid choice and distribution countries. Do not promise pricing in graphics.
2. Upload icon, feature graphic and eight phone screenshots in numbered order. Add the alt text from `asset-manifest.json`.
3. Paste listing text and public privacy-policy URL. Confirm the policy is readable without login.
4. Complete Data safety against the final signed release and every active track. Current code has local Room/DataStore records, no network permission, no notification permission, and no advertising or analytics SDK. Local processing alone is not developer collection; account separately for any data leaving the device, Android backups and user-directed sharing. Read Google's current definitions before submitting.
5. Complete App access: no online login or special access needed. Declare ads based on the final artifact (none enabled in this version).
6. Complete content rating, target audience and Financial features declarations yourself. This app records payments but provides no banking, lending, transfers or payment processing. Do not declare bank-account services based on the visual design.
7. Review developer verification and any closed-testing requirement shown for your account. Meet it before production access.
8. Upload the **signed** AAB to internal testing first. Check Play's pre-launch report. Test fresh install, rotation, keyboard, large text, dark theme, multi-team switching, expense splits, partial settlement/revocation and CSV import/export on real devices.
9. Test the schema 3→4 upgrade before promoting. Unsupported schema 1–2 upgrades now fail without erasing records; migration support is not claimed for those legacy schemas.
10. Resolve policy/quality findings and submit production release through your account. This kit does not publish the app or guarantee Play approval.
